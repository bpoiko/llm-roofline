## Notes

Free-form observations for this machine. Everything below this heading is preserved.

- **Use the 70 W+ charger.** A second sweep ran on a 30 W USB-C charger: the battery drained 60% -> 9% under GPU load while "plugged in", and MLX decode throttled ~40%. Those results were discarded too. `suite.py` now checks charger wattage.
- **Plug in before a sweep.** The first full sweep (2026-10-31) ran on battery: throttled as it drained, then macOS force-slept at 1% mid-run. All of that data was discarded; `suite.py` now refuses to measure on battery.

- `iogpu.wired_limit_mb` raised to 20480 (default would be ~16 GB). Without it the 27B (18.8 GB resident) doesn't fit on the GPU.
- Measured read bandwidth 260 GB/s = 95% of the 273 GB/s spec.
- The 27B falls out of the page cache between Ollama reloads, so each isolated run pays a 16–21 s reload (in `load_s`). A full 27B Ollama sweep spends roughly 10 minutes just loading.
- Close other GPU-heavy apps before a sweep; with 24 GB, memory pressure shows up as lower decode.
